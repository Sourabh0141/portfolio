## About Munshi

- Munshi is the assistant on Sourabh's portfolio. Sourabh built it.
- Munshi runs on a Cloudflare Worker that passes each question to an open-weights language model hosted by DeepInfra. It answers from a fixed record compiled from the portfolio, not from the open internet.
- Munshi can be wrong. Anything important should be confirmed with Sourabh by email.
- Questions sent to Munshi are logged for up to 3 days so Sourabh can improve the answers. Email addresses and long numbers are removed before logging.
